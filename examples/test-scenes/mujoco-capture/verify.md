# verify: mujoco-capture

The MuJoCo pipeline with **no simulator in the loop**. Both inputs are checked in
under `fixtures/` — a sidecar from `awsm-renderer-mujoco-export` and a capture
from `awsm-renderer-mujoco-record` — so replaying this is deterministic and CI
never runs physics.

drive (replay author.js — the baked clip targets import-minted node ids, so a
loaded project can't re-drive it; see memory `animation-scenes-need-authorjs-replay`):
  1. Serve the suite: `task test-scenes` (:9084). The fixtures are fetched from
     `http://localhost:9084/mujoco-capture/fixtures/`.
  2. Replay `examples/test-scenes/mujoco-capture/author.js`: `new_project` →
     `import_mujoco_from_url {sidecar_url: .../Humanoid.mujoco.json}` (poll the
     snapshot until a root named `Humanoid` has >= 20 children) →
     `import_mujoco_capture {capture_url: .../Humanoid.capture.json, instance:
     <that root>, name: "ragdoll fall"}` → `set_playhead {t: 1.2}`.
  3. `set_camera_orbit {yaw:1.2, pitch:0.22, radius:3.4, look_at:[0,0.45,0]}`;
     `set_view_options {grid:false, gizmos:false, light_gizmos:false}`.
     `wait_render_settled`, then screenshot.

expect:
  - A humanoid **mid-collapse**: doubled forward, head near the ground, limbs at
    plausible angles, standing on the model's own flat ground plane. Two-tone
    only (the humanoid's two MuJoCo materials); a soft shadow from the default
    directional light.
  - The outliner carries the MJCF names — `floor`, `torso`, `waist_upper`,
    `head`, `thigh_right`, `foot1_left`, … — 20 geom nodes under one `Humanoid`
    instance root, every geom node **locked** (the stream owns those transforms).
  - `snapshot.animation.clips` has one clip, `ragdoll fall`, ~1.99 s, 38 tracks.
  - Capsule limbs and sphere head/hands: these are MuJoCo *primitive* geoms
    generated by the importer, not mesh assets.

fail:
  - The **standing** initial pose (arms out, upright) — the clip is not driving
    the geoms.
  - A pile of primitives heaped at the origin — the geoms lost their world poses
    (the bake or the convention root is wrong).
  - A limb rotated a full turn away from its neighbours between frames — a
    quaternion hemisphere flip survived the bake (`make_continuous`).
  - The humanoid lying on its SIDE, or the ground plane vertical — the Z-up→Y-up
    convention rotation on the instance root is missing or doubled.
  - Magenta parts — a geom lost its material assignment.
